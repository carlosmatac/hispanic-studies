# Questions after the first meeting (2026-10-14)

What the meeting answered is in [`2026-10-14-decisions.md`](2026-10-14-decisions.md). These are the ones it opened, in the order they block work.

## Blocking the build

20. **Atlas Hispano.** Do you know the author, or Álvaro Durántez Prados? His maps carry no licence we can verify, so we are drawing our own from public-domain data. But an email from *you* asking to use or adapt them with credit — or better, a commissioned map from the man who coined *iberofonía* — would be worth more than anything we can trace. Want us to draft it?
21. **The 50 authors.** Your dataset tags 40 authors as `visual_priority: 1`; we would take those plus the ten whose canonical text is in your Priority50 list. Do you want to pick the 50 yourself instead?
22. **Caribbean / Miami.** Your image folder has essentially nothing for this setting (one photo of Albizu Campos). Do you have references, or do we build that register out of colour and type rather than imagery?
23. **The 267 authors include 35 marked "counter-tradition"** — people who argued *against* Hispanic unity. We plan to show them as such, with your legend's own disclaimer that inclusion does not imply assent. Confirm that is what you want, because it is the difference between a map of a debate and a list of allies.

## Content and claims

24. **Nothing in the acquisitions workbook is decided** — every decision field reads `Unreviewed`, "accepted for commissioning: 0". So the site will show the *programme* (ten series, the five-year plan) and the books that exist, not forthcoming titles. Is there anything actually scheduled we should announce?
25. **The proposed introducers and editors** in that workbook are real scholars who have not agreed to anything ("introducers, endorsers and partners are suggestions, not agreements"). None of those names will appear on the site. Flagging it because it would be an easy and serious mistake.
26. **Which books exist right now?** You said "ahora mismo yo tres libros" [36:03]. Titles, ISBNs, covers, where they are sold.
27. **The pamphlet** — *Hispanic Reunification: 200 Years of Theory and Praxis*. Final PDF? EN only or EN/ES?
28. **Substack.** You raised migrating it here and then parked it yourself ("da igual, eso es otro tema" [17:24]). Leaving it on Substack for now: confirm. Also, it has been quiet since Dec 2025 — is it coming back?

## Register

29. **"Militante" — how far?** We are reading it as: sober, dense, high-contrast, confident, no decorative motion. Not as: slogans, flags as wallpaper, or anything that reads as a party. Is that your reading too?
30. **The Cross of Burgundy and the Tercios** are also used by the Spanish far right. Still unanswered from the first list (#5) and it now matters for merch and for the Community block.
31. **Guadalupe.** She is the single most recognisable image for your US audience, and she is a devotional image. Our rule: full size, titled and dated, never as texture, pattern or effect. Does she appear on the site at all?
