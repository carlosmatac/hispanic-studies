## Corrections to the canon data

- **T002** José Cecilio del Valle: `life` should be **1777–1834** (born 22 Nov 1777, Choluteca), not 1780–1834. Source: es.wikipedia; BVMC authority 'Valle, José Cecilio del, 1777-1834'.
- **T001** Carta de Jamaica: 1818 is right as first *publication* (in English); written 6 Sep 1815; first Spanish printing 1833. Worth saying on the record.
- **T015** En torno al casticismo: 1895 is the essays' publication (La España Moderna); book 1902.
- **T022** the Gutenberg 'Latin America: Its Rise and Progress' is a translation of a different book (Les démocraties latines, 1912): do not link it as this work.
