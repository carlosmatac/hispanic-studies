## Positioning notes for design

- He is a **creator first**: what works is short video with him on camera. The site must convert video traffic (pamphlet signup) and feel like the "institute" behind the creator.
- Tone is provocative (memes, "WE WUZ AZTECS" thumbnails) and politically charged (Black Legend, José Antonio, "Reconquista Syndicate", Falangism listed as a current to study). The brand has to hold "Institute" seriousness and creator energy at the same time.
- Some of the symbols he uses (Cross of Burgundy, Tercios) are also used by far-right groups in Spain. Worth asking how he wants to handle that on merch.
