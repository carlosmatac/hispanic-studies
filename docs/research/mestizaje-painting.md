# The Mestizaje painting Beltrán asked for (2026-10-29)

Beltrán asked for a mother and child "más racionalizada" than Morlete's casta painting on the Mestizaje pillar: the indigenous mother holding a mestizo child and a macaw from his moodboard.

## The painting

- *Diceño de Chichimeca. Natural del Partido de PaRal* (catalogued *Mujer chichimeca*), **Manuel de Arellano, 1711**, oil on canvas, 105 × 80 cm. **Museo de América, Madrid, inv. 2000/05/02** (pendant: *Hombre chichimeca*, inv. 2000/05/01). The earliest known precursor of casta painting.
- Catalogue record: [CER.ES](https://ceres.mcu.es/pages/Main?idt=23111&inventary=2000/05/02&table=FMUS&museum=MAM). Literature: R. Romero Asenjo, *Anales del IIE* 72 (1998), pp. 143–148 ([Redalyc](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/369/36907205.pdf)); LACMA *Contested Visions* (2011) checklist no. 141.
- On show in Madrid until 8 December 2026 ("Retratar el mestizaje", Casa Museo Lope de Vega).

## Why it is not on the site

The painting is public domain, but the only photograph is the Ministry's (Gonzalo Cases Ortega, CER.ES), whose terms allow only personal or teaching, non-commercial use ([conditions](https://ceres.mcu.es/pages/issued.jsp)). It is not on Wikimedia Commons. Carlos's copy (an X download, 864 × 1152) is the same photograph.

**Decision (Carlos, 2026-10-29):** use Miguel Cabrera's *De español e india, mestiza* (1763, Museo de Historia Mexicana, Monterrey; Commons, PD-Art) now, and ask the Museo de América for permission (and a high-resolution file) to swap to Arellano later.
