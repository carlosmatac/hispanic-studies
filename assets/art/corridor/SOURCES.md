# `assets/art/corridor/` — the pictures hung in P · Tornaviaje

27 works, fetched from Wikimedia Commons with the artist, date, licence
and source page recorded for each. They hang on the walls of the corridor in
[`proposals/p-tornaviaje/`](../../proposals/p-tornaviaje/).

## Why these

The review of Beltrán's own image folder
([`docs/research/beltran-image-folder.md`](../../docs/research/beltran-image-folder.md))
found that his imagination is **virreinal and mestizo** — Guadalupe, castas,
biombos, the Cuzco school, the Mexico City Plaza Mayor — while our earlier
proposals leaned on peninsular Velázquez and Zurbarán. This set follows his
folder's subject matter and sources the originals properly, which is issue #37
done for the images this page actually needs.

## How they were prepared

Fetched at 1100px from the Commons API, centre-cropped to 620×800 and saved at
JPEG q76: they are seen small and in motion, so the whole set is under 3 MB.
Re-fetch at full resolution before anything ships.

## Rights

Most are two-dimensional reproductions of pre-1900 works and are in the public
domain. Where a photograph of the work carries its own licence (CC BY-SA, CC
BY, CC0), that licence is recorded below and attribution is owed to the
photographer as well as the painter. **Before production**, confirm each one
and add the attribution line the licence requires.

Devotional images (Guadalupe, the Immaculate Conception) appear here as framed
paintings hung at full size, which is how they are shown in a church or a
museum. Per [`docs/brand/imagery-and-rights.md`](../../docs/brand/imagery-and-rights.md)
they are never used as texture, pattern or effect — and Beltrán should still be
asked before they appear on a published page.
