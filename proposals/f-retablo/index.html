IHS eagle · gold, rays and grain · Unicorn Studio scene

The logo is the altarpiece.

Beltrán already owns the strongest asset: the Habsburg eagle in his logo. A baroque retablo (Seville Cathedral's retablo mayor, Santa Prisca in Taxco, Tonantzintla in Puebla) puts one sacred image at the centre of a field of gold and rays. Here the eagle takes that place, and the rays are the same resplandor as Guadalupe's mandorla and a monstrance. Zero new assets: it turns the existing brand into the hero.

Effect stack

  1. Gold eagle: custom 3D effect that extrudes a distance field of ihs-logo.svg, bevelled, polished gold, swaying and leaning toward the cursor
  2. God Rays from the bright gold: the resplandor, drifting slightly with the cursor
  3. Sparkle on the highlights: gold-leaf glints
  4. Vignette in burgundy-black, Film Grain
  5. On load: the gold lights up and settles forward in 2.8 s

How it is built

One scene in the Unicorn Studio design "IHS · Retablo", built through the Unicorn MCP and embedded with <div data-us-project> + the SDK (v2.4.2). Free plan: the Unicorn watermark stays until we publish from a paid account. Agents cannot upload SVGs to the media library, so the eagle is extruded from a distance field we generated from the vector logo (assets/unicorn/); with the SVG uploaded by hand, the built-in 3D SDF and Particle Formation (the eagle assembling from gold dust) become available.

Without Unicorn

Three.js ExtrudeGeometry from the SVG, PBR gold with an environment map, pmndrs post-processing god rays and a particle pass.
