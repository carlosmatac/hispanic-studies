The canon · fifty texts

A living argument, |not a reading list.|

Every text is placed by the kind of unity it presupposes. Hispanists have been monarchists and Marxists, liberals and nationalists — which is why the authors who argued against Hispanic unity are on the chart too, drawn with a broken outline.

Classification adapted from Gustavo Bueno, “España y América” (2001). Portuguese, Brazilian, Lusophone African, Asian, Indigenous, Black Atlantic, Equatoguinean and Filipino traditions remain historically distinct. Their presence on this chart does not imply their assent to a Hispanic or Iberophone project.
