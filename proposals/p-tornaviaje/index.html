The mission

A tradition that was forgotten, not refuted.

The Institute studies and defends Hispanic civilisation: its history, its Catholic inheritance, its mestizaje and its two languages. De Indiis Editions translates and republishes the canon of that argument.

The claim is not that this tradition was defeated. It is that most of it was never translated, and an argument you cannot read is an argument you cannot answer.
