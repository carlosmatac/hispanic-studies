The first globalization

Silver went west. Silk came east. A civilization crossed the Pacific.

  1. 1565Tornaviaje

    Andrés de Urdaneta finds the return route from Manila to Acapulco. The Pacific becomes a Spanish lake for 250 years.

  2. WestboundAcapulco → Manila

    American silver, missionaries, soldiers and settlers sail with the trade winds.

  3. EastboundManila → Acapulco

    Porcelain, silk and spices from China and Japan reach New Spain, and then Europe.

  4. 1815Last galleon

    Independence wars end the route. The Hispanic world fragments: the theme of our pamphlet.
