8 reales columnario · Mexico City mint · 1732–1772
Legal tender in the US until 1857

You have carried Spain in your wallet.

The 8-reales columnario (two crowned hemispheres between the Pillars of Hercules, PLUS ULTRA on the ribbons) was legal tender in the United States until 1857, and the pillars with their ribbon are one of the most cited origins of the $ sign. For English-speaking US Hispanics it is the perfect hook: the Hispanic world is already in their pocket. The coin turns under candle light and dissolves into a ledger of characters and back.
