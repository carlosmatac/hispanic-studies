Francisco de Zurbarán · Agnus Dei · c. 1635–40 · Museo del Prado
Move the cursor: you are holding the candle

A dark chapel, and you hold the light.

Spanish Golden Age chiaroscuro is the most "Spanish Baroque" thing we can put on a screen. Zurbarán's lamb, bound on the altar, is Christ as sacrifice painted with almost nothing but light and black. The page is that chapel: the visitor walks it with a candle. It works beyond the hero too: on Read and Books each essay can be lit by its own painting, which gives the archive a single, recognisable mood.

Effect stack

  1. Painting: Zurbarán, Agnus Dei (Prado, public domain), as a WebGL texture with mipmaps
  2. Relief from the painting's own luminance (two blur levels), no extra maps
  3. Candle: warm point light on the cursor, eased, with a slow flicker; drifts over the lamb when idle
  4. Vignette and fine grain in the same shader

Merch spin-off

A black heavyweight tee with the lamb in a single warm spot, and art prints of the relit painting.

Built with our own code

About 150 lines of WebGL2 in main.js: one full-screen shader, no libraries. The Unicorn version would use process_image to get a real normal map and its Point Light effect; the luminance trick is close enough for a pitch. With reduced motion the painting stays still and dim.

Without Unicorn

This page is the without-Unicorn version: WebGL shader, normal from luminance (or a precomputed normal map, e.g. Depth Anything), point light on the cursor.
