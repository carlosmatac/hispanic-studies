The first map of a global network.

Ribero drew the Carta Universal for the Casa de Contratación in Seville, the first scientific world map. Two centuries of fleets then turned it into a working system: silver from Veracruz to Seville, silk and porcelain from Manila to Acapulco. The page shows that the Hispanic world was a network, not a periphery, which is the Institute's whole argument.

Effect stack

  1. Map: Diego Ribero, Carta Universal 1529 (public domain), full width
  2. Routes drawn on a canvas overlay as dashed curves (hand-placed, approximate)
  3. Ships: dots with short wakes travelling the Flota de Indias and the Manila galleon, including the northern tornaviaje
  4. Ports pulse when a ship arrives: Seville, Veracruz, Acapulco, Manila
